Scope

This policy explains how the Health Plotter iOS app handles information when you install and use it. Health Plotter is a local health-data analysis tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or emergency services.

Information the App can read

With your permission, Health Plotter reads selected information from Apple HealthKit on your device:

  • sleep analysis, including asleep and in-bed intervals;
  • workouts and selected workout categories, including running, cycling, swimming, and strength training;
  • heart-rate variability (SDNN), resting heart rate, and one-minute heart-rate recovery;
  • respiratory rate and VO₂ max;
  • step count and active energy burned;
  • mindfulness sessions; and
  • workout duration, distance, and energy details when Workout details is enabled;
  • dietary caffeine and water entries only after you turn on the optional Dietary factors setting; and
  • sexual activity and alcoholic-beverage entries only after you turn on the optional Sensitive data setting.

The current App requests read access only. It does not write data to HealthKit. Apple controls each permission and intentionally does not tell apps whether a user denied read access to a particular type. A missing result can therefore mean that permission is off or that no source data exists.

How the App uses the information

Health Plotter groups available values by day, calculates local summaries, compares metrics on recorded and unrecorded factor days, and displays charts, counts, confidence intervals, effect sizes, evidence labels, and methodology notes. Days missing the metric or factor are excluded instead of being treated as zero.

When the device supports Apple's on-device language model and you ask for an explanation, the App can pass a sanitized aggregate summary to that on-device model. It does not pass raw HealthKit samples or sample dates.

The App does not use HealthKit information for advertising, profiling, unrelated marketing, or service eligibility. It does not sell HealthKit information.

Where information is processed and stored

The current App processes HealthKit data on your device. It does not require an account and does not include a cloud database, advertising SDK, or analytics SDK. It has no server endpoint to which it uploads your health history.

Small app settings—such as onboarding completion, your goal, lookback window, saved comparisons, the Dietary factors, Workout details, and Sensitive data choices, and whether an authorization request was completed—are stored locally in iOS preferences. The App does not create a separate local archive of raw HealthKit samples.

Apple may include app preferences in device backups or process information according to Apple's own services and terms. This policy does not change Apple's handling of HealthKit data inside Apple Health.

Optional dietary and sensitive inputs

Dietary caffeine and water are never read until you enable Dietary factors. Workout details can be disabled independently while workout presence remains available. Sexual activity and alcoholic-beverage entries are never part of the default comparison list; Health Plotter requests them only after you enable Sensitive data. Turning an option off removes its factors from analysis and prevents the App from querying that optional source on the next load. You can revoke the underlying permissions in Apple Health at any time.

Sharing and disclosures

Health Plotter does not disclose HealthKit information to third parties. If you choose to email support, your message, attachments, and email address are processed by your email provider and the publisher as needed to respond. Do not include a Health export unless support specifically asks for it.

The publisher may disclose information when required by law, valid legal process, or to protect rights, safety, or security. The publisher does not sell health information or share it with advertising platforms, data brokers, or information resellers.

Your choices and deletion

  • Grant or revoke individual HealthKit permissions in iPhone Settings and the Health app.
  • Leave Dietary factors or Sensitive data off, or turn either option off at any time.
  • Change or delete saved comparisons inside the App.
  • Delete the App to remove its local preferences and saved comparisons.

Deleting Health Plotter does not delete source records in Apple Health. Manage those records in the Health app. For support correspondence, you may request deletion subject to records that must be kept for legal or security reasons.

Retention and security

The App does not retain HealthKit data on a publisher-controlled server. Local preferences remain until you delete them, reset the App, or uninstall it. Support correspondence is retained only as reasonably needed to respond, maintain a support history, or meet legal obligations. The App relies on iOS, HealthKit, and your device's security controls; no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and the publisher does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If the law where you live uses a different age threshold, the applicable standard controls.
